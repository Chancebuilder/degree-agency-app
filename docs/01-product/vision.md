# Product vision

Degree Dean helps adult learners turn prior college credit, exams, and alternative credit into an honest remaining path at transfer-friendly institutions.

The product answers what the student already has, what can transfer, what still needs to be earned, which Tier A program uses those assets best, and what that remaining path costs in time and money.

The Degree Agency is not a college, accreditor, registrar, or degree-granting institution.
