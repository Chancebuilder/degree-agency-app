# Seed data scope

8 Tier A institutions, 22 bachelor's programs, 4 providers, 69 credit opportunities, 730 requirement slots, 262 policy rules, 1,686 equivalencies. Generated by `scripts/generate_seed.py`. Last verified `2026-09-01`.

Tuition, residency, and ACE/PLA posture cite official pages and are marked VERIFIED. Equivalency rows are compiled from those institutions’ published transfer posture plus common ACE-provider and community-college course codes. They are **not** registrar extracts and are **not** a guarantee of current practice.

Purdue Global’s self-paced bachelor’s path is **ExcelTrack** ($2,500 / 10-week term plus a $345 resource fee). **Purdue Global BS Professional Studies** is a separate open/individualized degree at $195 per month plus ASK assessment fees and the PR499 capstone — not the ExcelTrack per-term rate.

## Degree families

- `BUSINESS_ADMINISTRATION` — business bachelor's at every seeded school
- `CYBERSECURITY` — cybersecurity or the closest self-paced IT bachelor's (UMass Global MyPath BSIT)
- `PROFESSIONAL_STUDIES` — TESU BA Liberal Studies, Excelsior BS Liberal Arts, Charter Oak BS General Studies, SNHU BA General Studies, UMass Global BA Applied Studies Leadership, Purdue Global BS Professional Studies

UMPI YourPace and WGU do not publish an open professional-studies bachelor's in this seed, so they do not appear when that family is selected.

## How to regenerate

```bash
python3 scripts/generate_seed.py
```

Restart the API so `SeedLoader` upserts by stable UUID.
